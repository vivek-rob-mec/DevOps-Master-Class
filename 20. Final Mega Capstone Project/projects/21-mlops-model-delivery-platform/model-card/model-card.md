# Fraud Risk Model Card

## Intended use and prohibited use

Document the decision supported, affected users, human-review boundary, jurisdictions, and prohibited autonomous actions.

## Data and evaluation

Record immutable dataset references/checksums, feature lineage, temporal split, leakage review, accuracy/calibration, slice metrics, fairness review, robustness, latency, and limitations.

## Approval and operations

Record training run, source commit, artifact digest/signature, approvers, registry version/alias, monitoring thresholds, drift policy, retraining trigger, rollback owner, and retirement date.
